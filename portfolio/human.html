AI TRANSPARENCY

Human
first.

I use AI as a tool, not as a substitute for ownership. The goal of this page is simple: make the boundary visible.

AI can help me

Explore alternatives, explain unfamiliar concepts, generate boilerplate, spot edge cases, compare approaches and accelerate repetitive work.

I still own

The problem definition, product decisions, trade-offs, testing, verification, final implementation, presentation and responsibility for what I publish.

My anti-AI profile

I do not want a portfolio that sounds impressive but cannot be defended. Every project should be traceable to something I can explain, demonstrate or improve.

Proof over adjectives

Instead of saying “innovative” or “passionate” repeatedly, this portfolio prioritizes working projects, decisions, experiments and evidence.

AI makes the first draft cheaper. It does not make the thinking optional.
