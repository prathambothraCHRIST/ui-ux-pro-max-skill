PROFILE

The way
I work.

01 — CURIOSITY

I ask “why?” before “how?”

Understanding the reason behind a problem helps me avoid building a polished solution to the wrong problem.

02 — OWNERSHIP

I prefer working prototypes.

A working prototype exposes assumptions quickly. I learn more from an imperfect product I can test than from a perfect plan I cannot.

03 — COMMUNICATION

I make complexity visible.

Whether it is a financial model, a game system or a presentation, I try to turn complicated ideas into structures other people can navigate.

04 — GROWTH

I keep the feedback loop short.

Build → observe → change → repeat. The goal is not to be right on the first attempt; it is to improve quickly and honestly.
