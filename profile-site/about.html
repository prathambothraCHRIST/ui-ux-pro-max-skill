02 — ABOUT

A profile that is more interested in doing than performing.

I'm interested in the space between an idea and a working product: understanding the user, shaping the experience, building the system and learning from what happens next.

MY WORKING STYLE

Curious → practical → iterate.

I like projects where technology has a clear purpose. That can mean a multiplayer simulation, a useful student product, a clean interface or a system that makes a complicated process easier to understand.

I don't treat design as decoration. Structure, hierarchy, copy, interaction and accessibility are part of the product itself.

The goal is simple: make something people can understand quickly, use confidently and remember for the right reasons.
